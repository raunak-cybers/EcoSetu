Features

Everything E-Waste Needs

From informal pickup to certified recycling — every step is tracked, transparent, and compliant.

AI-Powered Classification
Point your camera at any e-waste item. Gemini AI identifies the material type, estimates weight, and suggests fair pricing instantly.
Batch & Lot Management
Collectors create batches of e-waste lots, each individually tracked with status, weight, condition, and value through the entire lifecycle.
Offline-First & Real-Time Sync
App works fully offline in remote areas for seamless pickups. Once connected, every lot and status change syncs live to the recycler dashboard.
Digital Audit & Lot Trails
Maintains verifiable records of all batches, materials, and transactions. Crucial for formal compliance and recycler audits.
Recycler Dashboard
A dedicated web dashboard to track incoming lots, quantify critical mineral recovery, enforce supply-chain traceability, and monitor formal compliance.
Multilingual Support & TTS
Full Hindi, Marathi, and English translations combined with built-in Text-To-Speech (TTS) ensures complete accessibility for all kabadiwalas.

How It Works

From Pickup to Recycling

A seamless 4-step flow that digitises the entire informal e-waste chain.

📸
Scan & Classify
Kabadiwala scans e-waste using phone camera. AI identifies material (PCB, Battery, Cable, etc.) and suggests value.
📦
Create Lot
Items are grouped into lots with declared weight, condition, and photos. Each lot gets a unique tracking ID.
🏭
Recycler Verifies
Recycler receives the lot on their dashboard, inspects weight/material, accepts or flags variance.
💰
Instant Payment
Once verified, payment is processed at CPCB-benchmarked rates. Full receipt and audit trail generated.
